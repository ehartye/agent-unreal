# Staged suite adoption

- [general] Inventory the existing consumer at one exact source revision before writing its manifest. Record every suite, required row, step kind, expected and observed count, verdict and evidence identity. Keep consumer-specific scenarios, budgets and adapters in that consumer.
- [general] Validate a candidate plan without execution first. V1 has eight kinds; a legacy script-test step has no arbitrary-command substitute. Keep unsupported rows visible in the comparison. A rejected legacy aggregate proves that its contract is unsupported, not that its original checks failed or that a replacement passed.
- [general] Exercise the disposable fixture through the separately invoked native driver before a consumer trial. Preserve failures and targeted repairs independently; a successful targeted recheck does not turn an earlier failed full aggregate into a passing run. Fixture acceptance establishes the integration path, not consumer equivalence.
- [general] Compare both runners at one fixed consumer revision with any adapter delta explicitly recorded. Require equivalent required checks and verdicts for every row; compare planned/executed counts in their declared units rather than matching totals. Explain schema, lifecycle, exit-convention, units and scenario differences; unavailable retained evidence remains unknown. Do not translate unsupported behavior into an invented pass or use changed counts to manufacture parity.
- [general] Keep the current consumer machinery operational while any required mapping or evidence differs. Propose migration only after the complete comparison supports it; consumer code removal is a separate change. Do not change CI policy or add engine acceptance to the dependency-free merge lane as part of adopting this skill.
- [general] Retained verification requires unchanged original paths and source/build products. [Remote jobs](unreal-remote-jobs.md) bind original-remote-path verification to returned evidence; downloading an archive alone cannot qualify it. Arbitrary archive relocation and hostile-producer attestation remain unsupported. A local qualification receipt alone establishes no remote lifecycle proof.
